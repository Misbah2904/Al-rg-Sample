Welcome to Food Paradise

MORE THAN A MEAL.
IT'S AN EXPERIENCE.

Nestled along Mitmita Road in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, AL-RG FOOD PARADISE was crafted to redefine family dining. We bring together wholesome culinary craftsmanship with an extraordinary open-air atmosphere surrounded by cooling water elements, tranquil landscaping, and the scenic Daulatabad foothill breeze.

Whether you are planning an intimate dinner inside our private illuminated gazebos, an evening celebration on our lush party lawn, or enjoying flavours with the kids at play, every detail is tailored for comfort, connection, and warmth.
