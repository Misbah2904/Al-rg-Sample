Helpful Details

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to common questions about visiting AL-RG FOOD PARADISE.

Q. Is there separate private seating for families?

Yes. We have dedicated private white-lattice family gazebos designed specifically to provide comfortable, private dining for families.

Q. Is parking easily available?

Yes. We offer spacious on-premise parking for four-wheelers and two-wheelers right near the main entrance.

Q. Do you serve both Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian food?

Yes, our kitchen serves an extensive multi-cuisine menu featuring both rich vegetarian delicacies and savoury non-vegetarian dishes.

Q. Is there a dedicated play area for children?

Yes, we feature a safe, open-air kids' fun play area where little ones can enjoy slides and play equipment while families relax.
