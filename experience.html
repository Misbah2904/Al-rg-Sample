FINE DINING ON WATER

Immerse yourself in our signature water dining atmosphere. Built alongside glistening blue water channels and fountain features, our seating brings you the soothing sound of moving water and cool breezes as you savour freshly prepared delicacies.

At night, underwater illumination and decorative lighting turn the water channel into a glowing promenade that transforms dinner into something truly extraordinary.
